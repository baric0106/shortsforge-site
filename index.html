ShortsForge

ShortsForge is a personal desktop tool that helps its owner make short vertical videos for their own YouTube channels. It runs only on the owner's own computer; it is not a public service and has no user accounts.

What it does with YouTube

When the owner connects one of their own YouTube channels and clicks "Upload to YouTube" on a finished video, ShortsForge uploads that video to that channel through the YouTube Data API. It also reads the public and private statistics of the connected channels (views, likes, comments, subscribers, video visibility) to show them on its own dashboard. Nothing is uploaded or read without the owner connecting the channel first.

ShortsForge uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.
