ShortsForge

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

ShortsForge is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. This page explains what it does with Google and YouTube data.

Data it accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, ShortsForge asks Google for two permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload a video to your channel, only when you click "Upload to YouTube".
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel's name, picture and statistics, and your videos' titles, visibility, views, likes and comments, so it can show them on its dashboard.

Where it is stored

The access token Google returns and the statistics it reads are stored only in files on the computer where ShortsForge runs. They are never sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's own YouTube API.

Sharing

ShortsForge does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.

Removing access

Click "Disconnect" in ShortsForge's Settings to delete the stored token from the computer. You can also revoke ShortsForge's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, as described in the Google security settings.

Google and YouTube

ShortsForge uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and your use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service.
